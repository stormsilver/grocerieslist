# Generate List

Generate List helps you turn your household product catalogue into a focused shopping list for one store. It captures the products marked as needed when you enter the mode, so you can track that trip's list even as you update each product's status.

## Make a list

1. Open the shopping list and choose the store you plan to visit.
2. Mark the products you need with their check circles. A checked circle means the product is already on hand; an unchecked circle means it is needed.
3. Select **Generate list**.

The list now shows the products that were marked as needed when you entered Generate List mode. This visible set stays fixed until you leave the mode, and applies across all three sections:

- **In order** shows products from the snapshot at this store in their saved shopping order.
- **Unordered** shows products from the snapshot at this store that do not have a saved place in the order.
- **Not at this store** shows products from the snapshot that are associated with another store, so you can see what may need to be picked up elsewhere.

## While shopping

When you find or buy a product, mark it as on hand by checking its circle. It stays visible in Generate List mode so you can keep your place and review what you have handled. Changes to the product's needed status are saved automatically and carry across the household catalogue and other store lists. The visible set updates the next time you leave Generate List mode and enter it again.

You can still organize the list while Generate List is active. Move a product into **In order** to add it to this store's shopping order; move it to **Not at this store** to remove it from this store's list. Products in **Unordered** can be placed in the order by moving them into **In order**.

## Show everything again

Select **Show all** to leave Generate List mode and see needed and already-on-hand products again. Your chosen store and saved shopping order remain available when you switch views.

Generate List is a display snapshot, not a separate saved list. It captures the products marked as needed when you enter the mode; changes to needed status do not change the visible set until you exit and enter again.
